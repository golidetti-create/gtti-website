Common questions about programmes, admissions, ODeL and qualifications.

Programmes

What can I study at GTTI?

GTTI's 2026 catalogue includes Social Work & Community Development, ICT & Digital Technology, Business & Management, Accounting/Finance/CPA, Criminology/Security/Justice, Beauty & Cosmetology, Counselling & Psychosocial Support, and focused short courses.

How do I choose a programme?

Start with your KCPE/KCSE result or existing qualification, identify an appropriate route, explore the relevant school and confirm the specific programme entry requirements with GTTI.

Admissions

What documents do I need?

Typical registration documents include National ID, Passport or Birth Certificate, previous academic certificates, transcripts or result slips where applicable, a recent passport-size photograph and programme-specific documents.

When are intakes?

Current intake dates are confirmed by the Institute before enrolment. Contact admissions for the latest verified intake information.

ODeL & Learning

Can I study online?

GTTI offers Open, Distance & e-Learning with structured academic support, scheduled online classes, recorded lessons, digital resources, assignments, CATs and academic/ICT support.

Is flexible learning completely self-paced?

No. GTTI's ODeL model provides flexibility while maintaining scheduled classes, activities, assessment windows and published deadlines.

Qualifications

Are short courses equivalent to CPA or KNEC/CDACC/KASNEB awards?

No. Short-course certificates are awarded by Golide; external qualifications are awarded or examined through the relevant body.

Where is GTTI located?

The supplied documents identify GTTI as being in Nairobi, Kenya. Contact the Institute for the verified physical address and directions.
