Professional Routes

KASNEB CAMS

Certificate in Accounting and Management Skills — KCSE D (Plain) / recognised certificate.

KASNEB ATD

Accounting Technicians Diploma — KCSE C+ / CAMS / approved certificate.

KASNEB CPA

Certified Public Accountants — KCSE C+ / KASNEB or recognised diploma.

What You Can Study

CPA Foundation

Financial Accounting; Communication Skills; Introduction to Law and Governance; Economics; Quantitative Analysis; ICT.

CPA Intermediate

Company Law; Financial Management; Financial Reporting and Analysis; Auditing and Assurance; Management Accounting; Public Finance and Taxation.

CPA Advanced

Leadership and Management; Advanced Financial Reporting and Analysis; Advanced Financial Management; Advanced Management Accounting.

Practical & Ethics

Business Data Analytics; Workshop on Ethics; Work Simulation where applicable.

Important Qualification Note

CPA is a KASNEB professional qualification. A GTTI short-course certificate is not equivalent to CPA or any KNEC, CDACC or KASNEB award.
