Who We Are

Golide Technical Training Institute (GTTI) is a technical and vocational training institution in Nairobi offering qualifications, professional pathways and focused short courses.

The Institute combines flexible Open, Distance & e-Learning with structured academic engagement and practical arrangements where programmes require hands-on training, supervised practical work or competency assessment.

At Golide, learning is designed around progress: understanding where you are starting, identifying the route that fits your goals, building competence and taking the next step.
