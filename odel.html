Simple Steps

How to start learning online

Follow these three steps to access your learning materials and begin using GTTI Moodle.

01

Sign In

Click a Moodle access button and enter the username and password associated with your account.

02

Open Your Course

Once logged in, visit your dashboard and select a course in which you are enrolled.

03

Study & Participate

Read the materials, follow course instructions, participate in activities and submit assignments as directed.

Be Ready to Learn

Everything you need to get started

You do not need an expensive computer to begin. A suitable smartphone and internet connection can help you access many Moodle learning activities.

  • ✓A device: a smartphone, tablet or computer.
  • ✓Internet access: a connection to open course materials and activities.
  • ✓Your login details: the username and password for your Moodle account.
  • ✓Commitment: check course announcements and assignment deadlines regularly.
?

Having trouble logging in?

If your login details do not work, use the password-reset option on Moodle if available. If you can sign in but cannot see your course, contact your institute’s designated Moodle administrator or student support team.

Try Logging In Again ↗
Helpful tip: Double-check your username and password. If you have forgotten your password, use the password-reset option on the Moodle login page if available.
