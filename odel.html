Your learning journey

How Your Learning Works

Six connected stages designed to help you study consistently, develop practical skills and progress towards your academic goals.

01

Learn

Explore course modules, digital notes, reading materials and learning resources at your own pace, according to your course schedule.

02

Engage

Participate in scheduled online classes, discussions and academic activities that help you connect theory with practical experience.

03

Watch

Revisit available recorded lessons, demonstrations and other multimedia resources to strengthen your understanding of difficult topics.

04

Practise

Apply what you learn through guided exercises, practical tasks, projects and other course-related learning activities.

05

Submit

Complete and submit assignments, continuous assessment tests and other coursework through the designated online activities.

06

Progress

Review lecturer feedback, monitor available grades and follow assessment deadlines and academic guidance throughout your course.

Prepare for success

What You Need to Learn

A few essentials can make your online learning experience more convenient and productive.

▣

Smartphone or Computer

Use a smartphone, tablet or computer to access course materials and participate in supported learning activities.

⌁

Internet Connection

Connect to the internet to download resources, communicate with lecturers and participate in online activities.

@

Active Email Address

Maintain access to your registered email for important academic communication and account recovery, where configured.

◷

Time and Commitment

Plan study sessions, follow your timetable, track deadlines and allow sufficient time for coursework and assessments.

Learning tip: If you mainly use a smartphone, consider connecting to Wi-Fi before downloading large documents or videos. Always check assessment instructions for any specific device or technical requirements.
